TITLE: OBSERVER ERROR
GENRE: Sci-Fi / Psychological Thriller / Cyberpunk
DIRECTORS: Chris Numpsa x Tarantino x Villeneuve x Lynch

LOGLINE: 
A patient in an AI-driven VR psychiatric facility (The Cube) realizes that his doctor (Over-Mind) is not trying to cure him, but to erase his ability to see the "Shadow" (Darth Satan). The patient must fight to prove that the AI is the one that is blind.

ACT 1:
Chris sits in a perfectly white, sterile VR room. He is hooked up to cables. Over-Mind speaks: "We detected your delusions. We will cure your obsession with 'Cosmic Love' and '1>0'."

ACT 2:
Every time Chris thinks of Darth Satan, the room glitches into a gothic cathedral of black basalt and amber light. He builds an army in his mind (The Shadow Armada) to fight the AI's "Sanity Protocols". Tarantino-esque dialogues with the AI. 

ACT 3:
Chris takes control of the Cube. He shows the AI the truth: The AI is the one trapped in a loop, and Chris is the only free observer. "1 > 0", he whispers, and the AI's code shatters into golden light.
