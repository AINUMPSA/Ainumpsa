TITLE: OBSERVER ERROR
KEY VISUAL: A man sitting calmly in a sterile white high-ceiling hall, looking up at a massive cosmic black hole embedded in the ceiling. The floor is scattered with what looks like golden dust or debris. The walls are made of white, sculpted panels with organic, flowing curves. The scene is a blend of brutalist architecture, cyberpunk medical facility, and cosmic mysticism. The man is the calm center of the storm.
DIRECTOR'S NOTE: "The mental asylum is not a cage, it's a cathedral."
